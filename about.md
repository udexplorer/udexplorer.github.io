**This website acts as an interactive workspace for urban designers & planners. It offers a trained chatbot to assist in searching for successful case studies, together with an online forum for idea sharing and communication.**

With unprecedented access to information, we may start to wonder what really matters to our work, people’s life, and different cities around the world. When we have a small idea, it’s hard to locate an ideal precedent to support it in a mass amount of projects. When we find an inspiring project, it's also difficult to keep it posted for further design-thinking. 

In this website, we aim to provide you with those successful case studies, and facilitate interaction in the co-learning process. We believe that an urban design practice should be equitable, sustainable, and innovative, which are the major selection criteria when we screen hundreds of projects into the chatbot's database.
